BUILT AROUND BETTER RESOLUTIONS

Support people, not just reply boxes.

Bring the useful parts of a support workflow together, while making uncertainty visible and keeping consequential decisions with the people who own them.

▣

Understand the queue

Organize tickets by topic, urgency, and status so agents can quickly find the conversations that need attention.

✦

Draft with context

Prepare a clear response based on the issue and available guidance, ready for an agent to edit and approve.

▤

Bring knowledge closer

This prototype uses fictional tickets and local, templated suggestions. It does not connect to order, payment, or support systems; it does not send customer messages or perform refunds or account changes. Real integrations would require authorization, verification, auditability, and testing.

WORKFLOW DESIGN

AI workflows

Assistive automation with explicit guardrails. The demo does not contact customers or perform real account actions.

Ticket classification

Suggest category, urgency, and intent from ticket content.

Knowledge-grounded draft replies

Draft a reply using the demo knowledge snippets; human review required.

Low-confidence escalation

Flag refund, legal, privacy, account-security, or unsupported requests for human handling.

Automatic send / refund / account changes

Disabled by design in this prototype. Keep high-impact actions behind authorization and approval.

Demo workflow rules are illustrative and do not execute external actions. Connect real systems only after authentication, permissions, audit logging, and testing are implemented.
